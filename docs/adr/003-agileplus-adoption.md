# ADR 003: Adoption of AgilePlus

## Status
Accepted

## Context
As the team scales and project complexity increases, our current development process has become a bottleneck. We need a framework that improves collaboration, provides better visibility into project progress, and manages changing requirements effectively.

## Decision
We will adopt **AgilePlus**, a hybrid methodology combining Scrum and Kanban, to manage our software development lifecycle.

- **Sprints**: We will work in 2-week fixed-length sprints to provide a regular cadence for planning and review.
- **Kanban Boards**: We will use Kanban boards to visualize workflow and identify bottlenecks in real-time.
- **Continuous Improvement**: We will conduct retrospectives at the end of every sprint to refine our process.
- **Metrics**: We will track velocity and cycle time to predict delivery timelines and optimize our workflow.

## Consequences
- **Pros**:
    - Better predictability and planning.
    - Increased team transparency and communication.
    - Ability to adapt quickly to changing requirements while maintaining focus.
- **Cons**:
    - Requires a culture shift and discipline to maintain the process.
    - Potential for overhead in meetings and ceremonies if not managed well.
