## Capacity Rules

- Calculate capacity from completed, reviewable work rather than pull-request count.
- Include maintenance and quality work in capacity planning.
- Re-estimate an item after a greater-than-2x variance.
- Use the trailing five completed sprints for forecasting after the baseline is populated.
- Interruptions are recorded as unplanned work and linked to the relevant issue or incident.
